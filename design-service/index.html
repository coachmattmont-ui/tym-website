The Problem

The $40,000 mistake we see every week

Most smart home projects don't fail at install. They fail at the prewire — months before anyone shows up with a screwdriver. By then, the drywall is up and your options are gone.

🔌

Wrong wire in the walls

Cat5 where you needed Cat6A. Speaker wire in the wrong locations. Conduit pulled to nowhere. Re-running anything later means tearing out drywall — or living with the limitation.

🧱

Speakers and screens in the wrong spots

Atmos heights aimed at a couch you'll never sit on. A TV mount where the morning sun glares right onto the panel. Decisions a professional integrator would catch in 90 seconds.

💸

$40k of gear that doesn't talk to itself

You bought the lights, the locks, the thermostat, the camera, the speakers — and now you have five apps, three remotes, and a system that fights you every day instead of disappearing into the background.

FAQ

Common questions

I'm not building yet — am I too early?
No, you're early at the perfect time. The best version of this work happens 3–6 months before drywall. If you're further along, we'll meet you where you are and tell you what's still possible.
Will this work with [Josh.ai / Control4 / Crestron / Savant / Apple Home]?
Yes. We design platform-agnostic — we'll recommend the platform that fits your goals, budget, and tolerance for tinkering. We have strong opinions but we're not married to any one brand.
What's the difference between this and the free Wiring Bundle?
The free Wiring Bundle (Quick Guide + Ebook) is general — it covers common runs and use cases for most homes. The Design Service includes a Custom Prewire Checklist built specifically for your floor plan, plus an itemized system quote matched to your goals. Different tools for different stages.
Do I have to buy hardware from you?
Not at all. The design is yours to take wherever you want. We DO offer hardware fulfillment with full design-fee credit if you go that route, because most clients find it easier than running it down themselves.
What if I just want one room — a theater, or a media room?
Add the Theater Add-On to a Blueprint, or upgrade to Whole-Home which includes a full theater design. If it's just one room and nothing else, message us and we'll quote it.
How long does it take?
7–10 business days for Blueprint and Blueprint + Strategy. 14 days for Whole-Home. Rush available — message us.
What happens if I'm not happy with the design?
We revise it. The design isn't done until you're confident handing it to your builder. If we genuinely can't get there together, we'll refund the design fee.
