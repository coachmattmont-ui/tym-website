Why Most Home Theaters Disappoint

You'll watch one bad movie in there and never sit down again.

Most home theaters fail in the same three ways. All three are decided before the first stud goes up.

📺

The screen is wrong for the room

A 110" screen in a room that demands 135". A 4K projector throwing onto a wall that can't handle the brightness. A seating row at the wrong distance for the panel size. All math, all preventable.

🔊

Atmos speakers in fake positions

Heights pointed at the wrong seat. Surrounds at the wrong elevation. A subwoofer in the corner because that's where the cable was. Atmos is unforgiving — and most installs get the geometry wrong.

💺

Seating you'll regret

Sight lines blocked by the row in front. Recline depth that puts row two into the back wall. A second row riser that wasn't planned at framing. Fixable for $200 if you catch it now. $20,000 if you don't.

FAQ

Common questions

My room is awkward — odd shape, low ceiling, or it's a basement. Will this still work?
Yes. Most rooms we design for aren't perfect cubes. Awkward dimensions are exactly where a real design earns its money — speaker positions and sight lines that compensate for the constraints, not pretend they don't exist.
Do I have to use Atmos? What about a simpler 5.1 or 7.1 setup?
No, we design for whatever configuration fits your goals and budget. Atmos is great when the room and budget support it. We'll be honest about whether your room is worth the extra speakers.
I want a media room, not a dedicated theater — does this still apply?
Yes. Half of what we design is multi-use media rooms — TV during the day, theater at night. We'll tell you what compromises that costs you and design for the priority you want.
What if I'm building a whole home with a theater inside it?
Our Whole-Home Plan ($1,997) includes both a complete smart home design and a dedicated theater design — usually the better value if both apply.
How long does it take?
7–10 business days for Visualization and Theater + Strategy. 14 days for Build Plan. Rush available — message us.
What if I'm not happy with the design?
We revise it. The design isn't done until you're confident handing it to your builder. If we genuinely can't get there together, we'll refund the design fee.
