The SmartHome Brothers

Built by integrators who actually wire houses.

We're Greg and Matt Montgomery — the SmartHome Brothers™. We started Tym in 2009 after years of doing custom AV the traditional way: trucks, ladders, drywall dust, the whole thing.

Somewhere along the way we figured out that the most valuable hour we spent on any project was the planning hour. So we productized it. Today we design smart homes, theaters, and lighting systems for homeowners and builders across all 50 states — and we never have to touch a ladder to do it.
