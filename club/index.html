FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from your YouTube channel?
YouTube is one-way: we publish, you watch. The Club is two-way and personalized. We answer YOUR question about YOUR house, on a live call or in the community channel. The Design Service is even more custom (we build a Custom Prewire Checklist for your home), but the Club is where you go for ongoing help and learning.
I'm not building yet — I'm still researching. Is this for me?
Absolutely. About a third of our members aren't actively building yet. They join to learn the landscape, narrow down what they want, and have a place to ask questions as they research. Better to start in the Club than to start three months into a build.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Monthly is month-to-month, cancel in one click. Annual has a 30-day money-back guarantee — full refund, no questions asked, if it's not what you hoped for.
Do I need to be technical to get value?
No. The Club is built for homeowners, not engineers. We explain everything in plain English — and the community is full of people at every level, including total beginners.
What if I want a full custom design for my home?
That's the Design Service ($497–$1,997). The Club gives you ongoing help; the Design Service gives you a custom plan. They work great together — and members get 20% off the Design Service.
Where does the community live?
On Skool — easy to use, mobile app, no Slack/Discord noise. Everything (community, courses, live calls) is in one place.
