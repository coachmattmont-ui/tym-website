What does an AWOL ultra short throw projector cost?

A simple four-piece setup — projector, screen, surround, and a media player. Here’s what it actually costs, and whether it’s any good.

Matt Montgomery
Sep 7, 2026 · 6 min read

I recently had the chance to set up an AWOL short throw projector in my basement to demo. The kids loved it, and I have to admit I was pleasantly surprised.

For years, we’ve spec’d out elaborate high-end theaters for clients. Top-tier projectors, surrounds with separate amplifiers, pre-processors, top-of-the-line speakers, and the finest video players on the market.

And while I love that kind of theater experience, watching my kids’ response was a reminder there’s still tremendous value in simple setups.

So what does it cost?

You’ll need four things:

  • A projector
  • A screen
  • A surround sound system
  • A media player like a Roku or Apple TV
Projector
AWOL projectors run $3,000–$4,500. I’d plan on the $4,500 Aetherion Max unless there’s a budget constraint.
Screen
AWOL has a traditional fixed screen at familiar price points. For an existing space, you’ll want one of their floor-rise models — it rolls up from the ground, and honestly it’s awesome. ALR or basic, 100–130", $1,500–$3,900.
Surround
You can use a third-party system like Sonos. To keep it simple, I’d use the Soundbase setup from AWOL. The projector acts as the center channel; the Soundbase gives you surrounds and subwoofers. $1,499 with one sub, $1,999 with two subs — a full 5.1.
Cabinet
You can set everything on the floor or an IKEA media cabinet. That’s the charm — it’s simple. If you want it to look really cool, AWOL’s custom cabinet hides the projector and screen until use. $3,999–$4,999 depending on screen size. That’s what I’d recommend.

Total package — my setup at home

  • 120" ALR floor-rise screen
  • Aetherion Max projector
  • 5.1 surround setup
  • Custom media cabinet
Shipped to your home $12,996 Best part: you can set it up and watch your first movie in under 30 minutes.

If you don’t need the custom cabinet, our turnkey Signature package is $9,997 and includes the Aetherion Max, 120" ALR floor-rise screen, and 5.1 surround.

Is it any good?

Yes. It’s really good. Surprisingly good.

I’ve lived in a world where media rooms are dedicated, no windows, $30k projectors, $100k surround systems. I’m not saying this compares to that. But having demoed and installed dozens of setups like that, I’m genuinely impressed with the experience.

You can install it yourself with no experience in under 30 minutes.

For a living room or dual-purpose media room — where your wife doesn’t want it to look like a theater room, but you and the kids want the big game or some Madden — it’s really tough to beat.

Honestly, it’s a game changer.

My kids haven’t stopped using it since we set it up.
