We took a few years off. Now we're back.

Where we've been, why we paused, and what's next — simpler, more fun, and all about sharing with you.

Matt Montgomery
Aug 15, 2026 · 2 min read

I took a few years off, and I'm diving back in, learning what's new, and sharing all of it with you. This should be fun — actually, fun is one of the priorities this time around.

A lot of you reached out asking where we've been. Honestly? We just burned out, for a lot of reasons. Much of it was self-inflicted, but not all. If you're really curious, you can read our latest post over on Instagram here.

Fun is one of the priorities this time around.

What's more interesting is that we're back — and really, I'm back. I'm intrigued with the intersection of AI and home tech, fascinated to see where it's all going, and genuinely excited to dive back in.

We've also simplified our approach a lot. If you're looking for the Design Service, just book a quick 15-minute call with me right here.

Wondering if we'll install your new system? We might — but honestly, we only plan to take on one or two installs a year now, and mostly for the fun.

The goal this time is simple: share what we learn with you, and design a few great media rooms along the way. Simple, hopefully helpful, and for sure fun.

Looking forward to seeing you here.
